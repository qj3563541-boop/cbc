    📖 Complete CBC Parameters Encyclopedia

    Detailed reference intervals and clinical significance for every CBC parameter.

    Hemoglobin (HGB)

    Male: 13.5-17.5 | Female: 12.0-15.5 g/dL

    The iron-containing oxygen transport protein in erythrocytes.

    • High: Polycythemia vera, COPD, chronic hypoxia, dehydration.
    • Low: Iron deficiency, acute blood loss, hemolytic anemia, thalassemia.

    Mean Corpuscular Volume (MCV)

    80.0 - 100.0 fL

    Average volume of a single red blood cell. Classifies anemia type.

    • High (>100 fL): Vitamin B12 or Folate deficiency, liver disease.
    • Low (<80 fL): Iron deficiency anemia, Thalassemia minor trait.

    RDW (Red Cell Distribution Width)

    11.5 - 14.5 %

    Measures red cell size variation (anisocytosis).

    • High: Iron deficiency anemia, mixed anemias.
    • Normal in: Thalassemia minor trait (uniform microcytes).

    ⭐ MID Cells (3-Part Analyzer)

    2.0 - 9.0 %

    Middle volume cluster: Combined Monocytes, Eosinophils, and Basophils.

    • High: Allergic conditions (asthma, eczema) or intestinal worms/parasites.
    • Suggested Action: Recommend Serum Total IgE and Stool R/E!

    Total White Blood Cells (WBC)

    4.0 - 11.0 x10^9/L

    Overall circulating leukocytes defending against infection.

    • High: Acute bacterial infection, sepsis, tissue necrosis, leukemia.
    • Low: Viral infections, bone marrow suppression, aplastic anemia.

    Platelets (PLT)

    150 - 450 x10^9/L

    Cell fragments crucial for blood clotting and hemostasis.

    • Low (<150): Dengue fever, ITP, DIC, acute leukemia.
    • High (>450): Reactive thrombocytosis, chronic inflammation.

    📊 CBC Analyzer Principles & 3D Slide Presentation

    Learn how 3-part and 5-part hematology analyzers operate using physical and optical principles.

    1. The Coulter Principle (Electrical Impedance)

    Blood cells suspended in an electrolyte diluent pass one-by-one through a microscopic aperture (approx. 70 µm). As a cell displaces conductive electrolyte, it causes a momentary rise in electrical resistance. The height of the generated voltage pulse is directly proportional to cell volume.

    2. Optical Light Scattering (5-Part Flow Cytometry)

    In 5-part analyzers, cells pass through a laser beam. Forward Scatter (FSC) measures cell size, while Side Scatter (SSC at 90°) measures internal complexity, cytoplasmic granules, and nuclear lobes, separating Neutrophils, Lymphocytes, Monocytes, Eosinophils, and Basophils.

    3. Hydrodynamic Focusing

    Sheath fluid surrounds the sample stream within a laminar flow nozzle. As the channel narrows, viscous forces stretch the blood cells into an exact single-file sequence, preventing two cells from passing together (zero cell coincidence error).

    4. SLS-Hemoglobin Photometric Method

    Replaces older toxic cyanide methods. Sodium Lauryl Sulfate (SLS) surfactant rapidly lyses erythrocytes and oxidizes heme iron to methemoglobin, creating an SLS-Methemoglobin complex measured at 555 nm absorbance.

    5. Summary: 3-Part vs 5-Part Analyzers

    • 3-Part Analyzers: Electrical impedance based. Separates Lymphocytes, Granulocytes, and MID cells (Monocytes, Eosinophils, Basophils). Cost-effective for general clinics.
    • 5-Part Analyzers: Laser light scattering based. Provides exact Neutrophils, Lymphocytes, Monocytes, Eosinophils, and Basophils breakdown.

    3-Part: LYM (30-85 fL) | MID (85-125 fL) | GRAN (125-350 fL)
    5-Part: Neutrophils | Lymphocytes | Monocytes | Eosinophils | Basophils
